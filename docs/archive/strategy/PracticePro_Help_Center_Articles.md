## GETTING STARTED

### Article 1: How do I create my first workspace?
**Category:** Getting Started
**Products:** Vega, Atrium, Komplete

1. Go to practicepro.ng and click "Start Free Trial" (Vega) or "Start 60-Day Trial" (Atrium).
2. Enter your email address and create a password.
3. Verify your email via the link sent by Brevo.
4. Choose your product: Vega (Legal), Atrium (Property), or Komplete (Both).
5. Enter your firm name, address, and phone number.
6. Select your practice area or property type.
7. Your workspace is ready. You'll land on the dashboard.

**Tip:** If you're a solo practitioner or small property manager, start with the free tier and upgrade as you grow.

---

### Article 2: How do I add team members?
**Category:** Getting Started
**Products:** Vega, Atrium, Komplete

1. Go to Settings > Team Management.
2. Click "Invite Team Member."
3. Enter their email address and select their role:
   - **Vega:** Founder, Admin, Lawyer, Paralegal, Portal User
   - **Atrium:** Founder, Admin, Property Manager, Accountant, Portal User
4. They'll receive an email invitation with a link to join.
5. Once they accept, they'll appear in your team list.

**Note:** Team member limits depend on your tier. Free/Starter = 1 user. Growth = 5 users (Vega) or unlimited (Atrium Pro). Check your usage meter in Settings.

---

### Article 3: How do I upgrade my subscription?
**Category:** Account & Billing
**Products:** Vega, Atrium, Komplete

1. Go to Settings > Billing & Subscription.
2. Click "Change Plan."
3. Select your new tier.
4. Choose billing frequency (monthly for Vega, annual for Atrium).
5. Complete payment via Paystack (card, bank transfer, or USSD).
6. Your new features activate immediately.

**Proration:** If upgrading mid-cycle, you'll be charged the prorated difference. If downgrading, the new rate applies at the next billing cycle.

---

### Article 4: How do I reset my password?
**Category:** Account & Billing
**Products:** Vega, Atrium, Komplete

1. On the login page, click "Forgot Password?"
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Check your inbox for a password reset link from Brevo.
4. Click the link and enter your new password.
5. Log in with your new password.

**Didn't receive the email?**
- Check your spam/junk folder.
- Ensure you're checking the correct email address.
- If you used a disposable email, you may need to contact support.
- Wait 5 minutes and try again.

## VEGA

### Article 5: How do I create a matter?
**Category:** Vega
**Products:** Vega

1. From the dashboard, click "New Matter" or go to Matters > Create.
2. Enter the matter title (e.g., "Chinedu Obi v. Lagos State Government").
3. Select the court and jurisdiction.
4. Choose the matter type (Civil, Criminal, Commercial, Property, etc.).
5. Add parties: client, opposing party, witnesses, counsel.
6. Set the filing date and expected hearing date.
7. Click "Create Matter."

Your matter now appears in the Matter Dashboard. You can link documents, set deadlines, and assign tasks.

### Article 6: How do I use DraftPro for legal documents?
**Category:** Vega
**Products:** Vega

1. Open a matter and click "Draft in DraftPro."
2. Choose a template or start from scratch.
3. The editor opens with A4 pagination and Nigerian legal fonts pre-loaded.
4. Use the toolbar to format text, insert placeholders, and add clauses.
5. Click the ALOA icon to generate AI-assisted content:
   - **Auto:** Quick suggestions as you type
   - **Flash:** Fast responses for simple queries
   - **Pro:** Detailed analysis for complex issues
   - **Research:** Comprehensive legal research
6. DraftPro's placeholder guardrails will warn you if you try to save with unfilled placeholders.
7. Export to PDF, Word, or print-ready format.

**Privacy:** All AI processing runs through PII Shield. Your client data is anonymized before reaching the AI model.

### Article 7: How do court date reminders work?
**Category:** Vega
**Products:** Vega

Court date reminders are available on Vega Growth, Pro, and Enterprise.

1. When you create a matter with a hearing date, reminders are auto-scheduled.
2. Reminders are sent via WhatsApp at:
   - 7 days before the hearing
   - 3 days before the hearing
   - 1 day before the hearing
3. Both the assigned lawyer and the client receive reminders.
4. Clients can confirm attendance by replying to the WhatsApp message.

**To customize reminders:**
- Go to Matter Settings > Reminders.
- Toggle reminder intervals on/off.
- Add custom reminder messages.

### Article 8: How do I set up the client portal?
**Category:** Vega
**Products:** Vega

1. Go to Settings > Client Portal.
2. Upload your firm logo and choose brand colors.
3. Toggle on "Enable Client Portal."
4. For each matter, click "Invite Client to Portal."
5. The client receives an email with login credentials.

### Article 9: How do I create an invoice?
**Category:** Vega
**Products:** Vega

1. Go to Billing > New Invoice.
2. Select the client and matter.
3. Add line items:
   - Time entries (auto-pulled from time tracking)
   - Fixed fees
   - Disbursements
   - Retainer deductions
4. Set payment terms (due date, late fees).
5. Preview and send via email or download as PDF.
6. Include your firm's Paystack checkout link for online payment.

**Retainer Billing:**
- Set up recurring retainer invoices in Matter Settings.
- The system auto-deducts from the client's trust account.
- Alerts fire when the retainer balance falls below your threshold.

### Article 11: How do I collect rent online?
**Category:** Atrium
**Products:** Atrium

1. Ensure Paystack is connected (Settings > Payments > Connect Paystack).
2. Tenants pay through the Resident Portal:
   - Log in > Click "Pay Rent"
   - Choose payment method: Card, Bank Transfer, or USSD
   - Complete Paystack checkout
   - Receipt auto-generates and emails to tenant
3. For manual bank transfers:
   - Tenant transfers to your firm account
   - Tenant uploads proof of payment in portal
   - You verify and mark as paid in Revenue Monitor
4. All payments auto-appear in the Revenue Monitor and tenant ledger.

**Dynamic Bank Details:** Your company bank details are pulled from the Founder Financial Hub. Update them there to reflect across all properties.

### Article 14: How does Sentry Pass work?
**Category:** Atrium
**Products:** Atrium

Sentry Pass is a visitor management system for gated properties.

**For Residents:**
1. Log in to Resident Portal.
2. Click "Sentry Pass" in Quick Services.
3. Click "Generate Pass."
4. Enter visitor name and purpose.
5. Choose validity: Single-use, 24 hours, or custom.
6. A 6-digit code and QR code are generated.
7. Share via WhatsApp, SMS, or copy.

**For Gate Security:**
1. Open the gatehouse terminal at /gatehouse?firmId=xxx
2. Enter the 6-digit code or scan the QR code.
3. Green checkmark = verified. Red X = invalid or expired.
4. The visitor's name, host, and validity display.
5. Check-in and check-out are logged automatically.

**Trial:** All Atrium users get a 14-day free trial. After trial, Sentry Pass is ₦15,000/month as an add-on.

### Article 15: How do I track service charges?
**Category:** Atrium
**Products:** Atrium

1. Go to Property Settings > Core Services.
2. Enable "Service Charge" for the property.
3. Define SC categories: Security, Cleaning, Generator Fuel, Waste, etc.
4. Set SC amount per unit (or prorate by unit size).
5. SC appears automatically on tenant invoices.
6. Tenants see itemized SC breakdown in their portal.

**Electricity Sub-Metering:**
1. Enable "Electricity" in Core Services.
2. Log monthly meter readings per unit.
3. The system calculates consumption and cost.
4. Compare usage across units to identify anomalies.

**Bulk Billing:**
- Create one SC invoice and distribute across all units automatically.
- Prorate by unit size or occupancy.
- Auto-calculate arrears and apply to next cycle.

## SECURITY & PRIVACY

### Article 16: Is my data secure?
**Category:** Security & Privacy
**Products:** Vega, Atrium, Komplete

Yes. PracticePro implements multiple layers of security:

**Data Isolation:**
- All data is isolated by firmId (workspace ID).
- No other firm can access your data.

**Encryption:**
- Data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Data encrypted at rest (AES-256)

**Access Controls:**
- Role-based permissions (Founder, Admin, Lawyer, etc.)
- Optional two-factor authentication (2FA)
- Recovery codes for account recovery

**AI Privacy:**
- PII Shield strips personal data before AI processing
- AI models are not trained on your data
- Workspace isolation prevents cross-firm data leakage

**Compliance:**
- NDPA 2023 compliant
- Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookie Policy, and DPA available

### Article 17: How do I enable two-factor authentication?
**Category:** Security & Privacy
**Products:** Vega, Atrium, Komplete

1. Go to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication.
2. Click "Enable 2FA."
3. Scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, Authy, or Microsoft Authenticator.
4. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
5. Save your 10 recovery codes in a secure location.
6. 2FA is now active.

**Enforced 2FA:**
- Admins can require all team members to enable 2FA.
- 2FA is required for sensitive actions: password changes, bank detail updates, data export.

## API & INTEGRATIONS

### Article 18: How do I connect Paystack?
**Category:** API & Integrations
**Products:** Atrium

1. Go to Settings > Payments > Paystack.
2. Enter your Paystack public key (test or live).
3. For live payments, ensure VITE_PAYSTACK_PUBLIC_KEY is set in production environment.
4. Test with a ₦100 transaction in test mode.
5. Toggle "Enable Live Payments" when ready.

**Payment Methods:**
- Card (Visa, Mastercard, Verve)
- Bank Transfer (instant account number)
- USSD (for non-smartphone users)

**Manual Bank Transfer:**
- Your company bank details are pulled from Founder Financial Hub.
- Tenants upload proof of payment.
- You verify and reconcile in Revenue Monitor.

**Category:** Account & Billing
**Products:** Vega, Atrium, Komplete

1. Go to Settings > Data & Privacy > Export Data.
2. Choose what to export:
   - Matters and documents (Vega)

### Article 20: What happens when I hit my storage limit?
**Category:** Account & Billing
**Products:** Vega, Atrium, Komplete

**Warning at 80%:**
- Yellow banner appears: "You're approaching your storage limit."
- Option to upgrade or buy add-on storage.

**At 100%:**
- Red banner: "Storage limit reached. 7-day grace period."
- You can still view and download existing files.
- You cannot upload new files until you upgrade.

**Storage Equivalents:**
- 1 GB ≈ 200 documents
- 20 GB ≈ 4,000 documents
- 100 GB ≈ 20,000 documents

**To free up space:**
- Delete old or duplicate files
- Archive closed matters (Vega)
- Compress images before upload

**Add-On:**
- Extra 50 GB: ₦8,000/month
