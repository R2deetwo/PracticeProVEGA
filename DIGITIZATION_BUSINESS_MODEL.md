
# PracticePro Legacy Intelligence Activation
## Business Model & Operational Workflow

This document outlines the strategy for the **"Legacy Intelligence Activation"** service—a premium offering designed to ingest offline history into the PracticePro ecosystem.

---

### 1. The Value Proposition

**"Your Firm's History, Activated."**

Most Nigerian law firms sit on decades of intellectual property locked in physical files. This knowledge is dormant.
**The Solution:** We don't just "digitize." We **ingest, index, and activate**. We turn physical archives into a private RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) database, giving the firm an "Institutional Memory" that ALOA can query.

---

### 2. Strategic "Stickiness" (The Moat)

This service is designed to create high switching costs for the client:
1.  **The Knowledge Graph:** Once we index 10 years of their case law, PracticePro becomes the only place they can ask: *"What precedents have we used for Land Use Act cases in Rivers State?"* Moving to another software means losing this "Super-Search."
2.  **Data Gravity:** The more data they pay to put in, the less likely they are to leave.
3.  **Subscription Uplift:** Large archives require the **Ultimate** plan for storage and vector processing limits, driving higher ARR.

---

### 3. Service Tiers

#### A. Self-Serve Ingestion
*   **Target:** Individual practitioners.
*   **Mechanism:** User uploads PDFs to Research Studio.
*   **Cost:** Free (included in subscription).

#### B. Concierge Activation (White-Glove)
*   **Target:** Established firms with physical file rooms.
*   **Mechanism:** PracticePro manages logistics, scanning, and data entry.
*   **Cost:** Project-based (Setup Fee + Volume Fee).

---

### 4. Costing Model (Concierge)

Pricing is structured to reflect **Value**, not just **Cost**.

#### Item 1: Intelligence Setup Fee (One-Time)
*   **Price:** **₦250,000** flat fee.
*   **Value Justification:** Setup of private vector database, encryption keys, and AI training context.

#### Item 2: Ingestion Unit Cost (Volume)
*   **Price:** **₦50,000 per Standard Bankers Box** (approx 2,500 pages or 5 reams).
*   **Includes:**
    *   Logistics (Pickup/Return).
    *   Professional unbinding/rebinding.
    *   OCR (Optical Character Recognition).
    *   AI Metadata tagging (Client, Suit No, Year).

#### Item 3: On-Site Surcharge (Optional)
*   **Price:** +20% on total bill.
*   **Condition:** For firms that require scanning to happen within their premises.

---

### 5. FAQ for Clients

**Q: Is my legacy data secure?**
A: Yes. Data is encrypted at rest. The AI model processes data via API but does *not* train on your confidential client data for the public model.

**Q: Can I just scan them myself?**
A: You can, but standard scanning creates "flat" images. Our process creates "intelligent" text that the AI can read, understand, and cross-reference.

**Q: Who owns the data?**
A: You retain 100% ownership of the raw files. We are simply the custodians of the digital index.
