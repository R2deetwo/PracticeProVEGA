COMMERCIAL LEASE

This Lease is made on 12 October 2026 between Iye Estates Limited (the "Landlord") of Lagos and Kola Tradings Limited (the "Tenant") of Lagos.

1. Demise. The Landlord demises the Premises to the Tenant for a term of three years from the date hereof.

2. Rent. The Tenant shall pay twenty-four months' rent in advance on execution of this lease, and monthly thereafter.

3. Repair. The Tenant shall keep the Premises in good repair throughout the term.

4. Termination. The Landlord may terminate this lease at any time without notice if the Tenant is in breach.

5. Indemnity. The Tenant shall indemnify the Landlord against all claims howsoever arising out of the Tenant's use of the Premises.

6. Use. The Premises shall be used for wholesale trading and for no other purpose.
