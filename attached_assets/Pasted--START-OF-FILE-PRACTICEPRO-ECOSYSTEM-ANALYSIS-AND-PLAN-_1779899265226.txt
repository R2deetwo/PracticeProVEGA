1. SITE AUDIT: EXISTING DEFICIENCIES & COMPONENT ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
An analysis of the current "bug standard" landing page reveals that it suffers from structural and experiential friction. Instead of commanding authority, it blends into standard SaaS "slop."

A. The Core Index Page (/)
- Issue: Audience Dilution. Trying to sell real estate automation (Atrium) and legal case workflows (Vega) on a single homepage dilutes your message. A lawyer looking for litigation tracking is confused by rent collection metrics; a property developer loses interest when wading through court hierarchy features.
- Visual Strain: The layout relies on symmetric blocks that fail to direct the eye, causing high bounce rates.

B. The Sub-Pages & Internal Routing
- Issue: Structural Bleed. Because the products are not properly segmented at the front door, the internal sub-pages feel tangled. This lack of isolation forces users to navigate through irrelevant features to find tools specific to their industry.

C. Primary Interaction Failure: The Registration CTA
- Issue: The 2-3 Second Lag. Clicking the "Create Account" or onboarding button results in a devastating multi-second delay. 
- Technical Cause: The system is blocking the UI thread by attempting to lazy-load heavy authentication bundles, initialize complex client-side states, or fire network requests at the exact millisecond of the click event. This delay signals an unoptimized app and acts as an immediate deterrent.

D. The Loading Experience (Splash Screen)
- Issue: Uncalibrated Timing. The splash sequence operates as an arbitrary loading spinner rather than a premium, cinematic transition. It feels like a performance bottleneck rather than an intentional branding element.

---

2. THE STRUCTURAL SOLUTION: THE HUB-AND-SPOKE ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
To fix user confusion and ensure visitors instantly get exactly what they need, the site must be refactored into a clear, audience-segmented routing system under the PracticePro corporate identity.
