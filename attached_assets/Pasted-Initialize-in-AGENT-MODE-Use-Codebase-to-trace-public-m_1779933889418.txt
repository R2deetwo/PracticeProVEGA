### COOKIE POLICY
**Last Updated: May 2026**

PracticePro Legal Technologies Limited ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on the Vega platform to ensure high-fidelity UI performance, verify active user sessions, and maintain secure database routing states. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your rights to manage their behavior.

#### 1. What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files downloaded to your browser or device when you interact with our platform. They allow us to recognize your device, maintain security contexts, and preserve your application dashboard preferences across browser sessions.

#### 2. How We Use Cookies
We categorize our tracking usage into three explicit execution tiers:
* **Essential/Strictly Necessary Cookies:** These are vital to power the secure core engine of Vega. They handle JWT authentication validation, route verification tokens, and preserve state continuity across internal database calls (e.g., maintaining workspace context when navigating between Matters and Contacts). The platform cannot function without these cookies.
* **Performance & Analytics Cookies:** These anonymous trackers monitor performance thresholds, database response lag times, and tracking artifacts. They help us identify visual layout anomalies or broken viewport states across different devices.
* **Functional Preferences:** These cookies record your UI workspace custom state choices, such as persisting sidebar toggles, table density parameters, or your theme configuration (e.g., Platinum UI dark-mode vs. light-mode layout setups).

#### 3. Specific Telemetry and Session State Keys Utilized
Our platform explicitly monitors local file parameters to preserve system configuration continuity:
* `__session` / Auth Tokens: Verifies active user profile claims and restricts data exposure strictly to authenticated team boundaries.
* `ui-state-preference`: Tracks workspace configuration options to eliminate loading layout flickers on page navigation.

#### 4. Managing and Controlling Cookie Preferences
You possess the absolute legal right to accept or decline non-essential tracking cookies. You can configure your internet browser to refuse or erase cookies globally via its built-in privacy settings. Please note that blocking or scrubbing essential tokens will instantly terminate your active session context and paralyze your ability to run database mutations or access secure case file paths inside the application.

For detailed legal inquiries regarding our tracking mechanics, please contact our data compliance desk at: `compliance@practicepro.esq`.
