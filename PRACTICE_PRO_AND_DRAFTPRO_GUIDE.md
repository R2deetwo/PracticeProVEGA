
# PracticePro & DraftPro: Comprehensive Feature Guide

Welcome to the definitive guide for **PracticePro**, the all-in-one legal practice management system designed for the modern Nigerian law firm, and **DraftPro**, its advanced legal word processor.

---

## Part 1: PracticePro Platform Overview

PracticePro consolidates your firm's operations into a single, intelligent workspace.

### 1. Dashboard (Mission Control)
The Dashboard provides a real-time pulse of your firm.
*   **KPI Cards:** Track Active Matters, Overdue Tasks, Outstanding Revenue, and Client Count instantly.
*   **Daily Intelligence:** The "Daily Focus" bar uses AI to suggest your next best action (e.g., upcoming hearings or critical deadlines).
*   **Activity Feed:** Monitor recent actions taken by your team on matters and documents.

### 2. Matter Management
Matters are the core unit of PracticePro.
*   **Lifecycle Tracking:** Move cases through customizable workflow stages (e.g., Intake -> Pleadings -> Trial).
*   **Jurisdiction Logic:** When creating a Civil Litigation matter, the system suggests the correct court (Federal vs. State High Court) based on Nigerian constitutional principles.
*   **Linked Data:** Every matter acts as a container for its specific documents, tasks, invoices, and calendar events.

### 3. Task Management & Workflows
*   **Kanban Board:** Visualize tasks by status (Todo, In Progress, Done).
*   **Automated Deadlines:** When you log a "Service of Process" event, the system calculates the filing deadline based on Nigerian Court Rules (e.g., 42 days for Statement of Defence in Lagos).
*   **Checklists:** Apply standardized checklists to matter stages to ensure no procedural step is missed.

### 4. Billing & Finance
*   **Time & Expense Tracking:** Log billable hours and disbursements directly against a matter.
*   **Scale Fees:** Use the built-in calculator to determine standard legal fees for property transactions based on the Remuneration Order 2023.
*   **Invoicing:** Generate professional PDF invoices with one click.
*   **Trust Accounting:** Manage client funds separately with dedicated trust deposit requests and balance tracking.

### 5. Research Studio
Your digital war room for case preparation.
*   **Notebooks:** Organize research by topic or case.
*   **AI Analysis:** Upload PDFs or paste text. The AI can generate Chronologies, Legal Matrices (IRAC), and identify Discovery Gaps.
*   **Nigerian Law Reports:** Access a built-in library of locus classicus cases (e.g., *Savannah Bank v. Ajilo*) with AI-generated summaries and ratio decidendi.

### 6. Client Portal
A secure, external-facing view for your clients.
*   **Share Documents:** Securely share drafts for review.
*   **Status Updates:** Clients can see the current stage of their case without calling you.
*   **Intake:** Prospective clients can submit case details (including voice memos) via a secure link.

## Part 2: DraftPro (Advanced Legal Word Processor)

DraftPro is not just a text editor; it is a **legal drafting environment** integrated directly into your case files.

### Key Features

#### 1. Context-Aware Drafting
DraftPro knows which matter you are working on.
*   **Auto-Fill:** It automatically injects client names, addresses, and suit numbers into templates using placeholders like `[CLIENT NAME]` or `[SUIT NUMBER]`.
*   **AI Co-Pilot:** Click the "Assistant" button to open ALOA alongside your document. You can ask it to "Draft a clause for Force Majeure" or "Rewrite this paragraph to be more concise."

#### 2. Legal Formatting Tools
*   **Page Layout:** Toggle between A4 and Letter, Portrait and Landscape. Adjust margins visually with the ruler.
*   **Professional Styling:** Supports standard legal formatting including numbering, bullets, indentation, and alignment.
*   **Letterhead Mode:** Toggle your firm's official letterhead overlay to preview exactly how the final PDF will look.

#### 3. Smart Templates
*   **Clause Library:** Quickly insert standard clauses (Severability, Jurisdiction, Dispute Resolution) with a single click from the "Tools" panel.
*   **Variable Detection:** The editor highlights any `[PLACEHOLDERS]` in yellow, ensuring you don't miss filling in critical details before export.

#### 4. Review & Collaboration
*   **Version Control:** Every time you save, a new version is created. You can compare versions side-by-side to see what changed (Redlining).
*   **Comments:** Add internal comments for your team directly on the document record.
*   **Magic Rewrite:** Select text and use the "Magic Rewrite" tool to instantly polish grammar or change the tone.

### How to Use DraftPro

1.  **Open:** Navigate to a Matter > Documents, and click "Draft New". Or, open an existing document and click "Edit".
2.  **Compose:** Type directly or use ALOA to generate initial text.
3.  **Format:** Use the ribbon toolbar for styling. Use the "Layout" tab to adjust page settings.
4.  **Refine:** Use the "Tools" panel to insert standard clauses. Click "Magic Rewrite" to polish selected text.
5.  **Save:** Click "Save" to version the document in the matter file. You can then download it as a PDF or share it with the client.
