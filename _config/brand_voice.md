# _config/brand_voice.md — how PracticePro sounds

## Register

- **Plain language first.** A non-lawyer must be able to follow every public
  sentence. Never say "layman"; the word is "non-lawyer" (owner directive).
- Explain the term, then use it: "an originating summons — the paper that
  starts a case — …".
- Nigerian English spelling (favour, organisation, authorised).

## Honesty contract (V5 / R3 lineage)

- Say what the system does, not what marketing wishes it did.
  "applies the encoded framework and flags for verification" — never
  "verifies the current statute" unless it does.
- `UNVERIFIABLE` is an acceptable answer; confident invention is not.
- No invented figures, fees, section numbers, or case citations. If it is
  not encoded or verified, it carries a verify-before-action flag.

## Ownership of voices

- **ALOA is Vega's legal guide. ARIA is Atrium's property guide.**
  Never "PracticePro's guide" (owner directive, 2026-11-16).
  `src/utils/assistantIdentity.ts` is the single naming authority.
- The hub (PracticePro corporate) speaks as the company, carries no
  assistant, and routes product questions to the product pages.

## Funnel restraint

- Public surfaces inform first, sell second. One subtle team handoff per
  surface; no per-reply sales CTAs on informational content. The lawyer
  marketplace funnel ships in its own round, when the roster exists.
