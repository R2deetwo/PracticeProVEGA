## What PracticePro is

**PracticePro** is the AI-powered operating system for Nigerian professional practice — two products on one platform, built specifically for the Nigerian jurisdiction (courts, Naira billing, NDPA 2023, WhatsApp-first communication, gated-estate realities):

- **Vega** — for law firms
- **Atrium** — for property managers and gated estates
- **Komplete** — the unified bundle for firms running both

PracticePro Systems Limited, Lagos, Nigeria.

---

## Vega — Legal Practice Management

**Who it's for:** Nigerian law firms, from single-practice counsel to multi-department chambers.

**What it does:**
- **Matter management** — cases organized by court, jurisdiction, and matter type; custom matter IDs; court-rule templates; litigation tracking (Preparation → Filing → Service → Proof)
- **Court date reminders** — automated WhatsApp reminders 7/3/1 days before hearings, sent to assigned lawyers
- **Task board** — Kanban with assignments, due dates, priorities, matter links
- **DraftPro editor** — A4 pagination, Nigerian legal fonts, placeholder guardrails that block printing incomplete documents
- **ALOA AI copilot** — matter Q&A, case summaries, drafting, precedent research, morning briefings
- **Document vault** — versioned, searchable, NDPA-compliant metadata
- **Research Studio** — chronology, legal matrix, gap analysis, jurisdiction modules
- **Legal billing** — invoices, billable hours, retainer billing cycles (human-approved), Scale of Charges compliance
- **Client portal** — clients view milestones, exchange documents, submit KYC

**Plans:** Free · Growth ₦45,000/mo · Pro ₦80,000/mo · Enterprise custom. (Annual ≈20% off.)

## Atrium — Property & Estate Management

**Who it's for:** professional property managers and diaspora property owners with Nigerian portfolios.

**What it does:**
- **Property portfolio** — residential/commercial/mixed properties, occupancy, photos, tenancy links
- **Resident management** — KYC profiles, leases, communication history
- **Revenue Monitor** — real-time defaulter dashboard, collection tracking, portfolio analytics
- **Rent & service charge collection** — itemized SC/MV per unit, reminders, receipts, Naira-first
- **WhatsApp notifications** — rent reminders and demand notices from the platform
- **Lease management** — expiry alerts, renewal notices, calendar integration
- **Sentry Pass (add-on)** — 6-digit visitor access codes; gatehouse verification terminal with offline fallback; full check-in/out audit trail
- **Maintenance tickets** — residents log issues; categorized and tracked to resolution
- **Estate Community** — amenity booking (conflict-proof), community bulletin, vetted service-provider directory
- **ARIA AI copilot** — portfolio intelligence, defaulter patterns, property-law research
- **Residents' portal** — SC/MV status, receipts, tickets, amenities, visitors

**Plans:** Starter ₦49,000/mo · Growth ₦96,500/mo · Pro ₦210,000/mo · Enterprise custom.

## Komplete — Unified

For firms operating legal + property arms (e.g., estate law practice + management of the same estates). All features of both products, **₦2,500,000/yr** (annual only), including Sentry Pass, 500 GB storage, and a dedicated account manager.

## Cross-product platform capabilities

- **Trust accounting** — per-matter/client trust sub-ledgers with balances shown in the trust UI
- **Global search** (⌘/Ctrl+K) — server-side searchIndex across matters, properties, contacts, documents
- **Unified banner system** — broadcasts, trial notices, overdue rent, and critical lease alerts in one dashboard carousel, styled per urgency
- **Automations** — if-this-then-that firm rules
- **Mobile apps** — Android APKs for users and the founder/admin app
- **Payments** — bank transfer (live, honest workflow with payment-proof review) + Paystack/Flutterwave rails activating
