
# PracticePro Sales Playbook: Legacy Intelligence Activation

**Internal Use Only**

## The Pitch
> "You have 20 years of experience sitting in filing cabinets. Currently, that experience is dormant. We don't just scan your files; we **activate** them. We turn your physical archive into a searchable AI brain that your junior lawyers can query instantly."

---

## 1. The "Stickiness" Strategy (Why they won't leave)
When selling, emphasize that **PracticePro learns from their history**.
*   **The Hook:** "The longer you use PracticePro, and the more legacy data you ingest, the smarter ALOA becomes about *your* specific style of advocacy."
*   **The Lock-In:** Once their 5,000 past cases are indexed in our Vector Database, moving to a competitor means lobotomizing their firm. They lose their "institutional memory."

---

## 2. Volume Estimator (How to Quote)
Lawyers rarely know how many "boxes" they have. Use this guide to estimate for them during the walkthrough.

| Physical Unit | Est. Boxes | Est. Pages | Quote Estimate (approx) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Bankers Box** (approx 2,500 pages) | 1 | 2,500 | ₦50,000 |
| **Vertical Filing Cabinet (4 Drawer)** | 8 - 10 | 25,000 | ₦400,000 - ₦500,000 |
| **Lateral Filing Cabinet (Wide)** | 12 - 15 | 35,000 | ₦600,000 - ₦750,000 |
| **Open Shelf (3ft wide, full)** | 3 - 4 | 9,000 | ₦150,000 - ₦200,000 |
| **"Small" Archive Carton** | 0.8 | 2,000 | ₦40,000 |

*Note: Always round UP for safety margins.*

---

## 3. Pricing Structure
Do not just quote a per-box fee. It sounds cheap and transactional. Structure the quote as a **Digital Transformation Project**.

### A. System Activation Fee (Mandatory)
**Price: ₦250,000 (One-off)**
*   **What you tell the client it covers:** "Configuration of your private encryption silo, AI vector database setup, and custom prompt engineering for your firm's specific document types."
*   **Real Value:** This is pure margin and commitment fees.

### B. Processing Fee (Variable)
**Price: ₦50,000 per Standard Bankers Box**
*   **What it covers:** Logistics, unbinding, high-speed OCR scanning, re-binding, and data cleaning.

### C. Storage & Hosting (Recurring)
**Price: Included in Ultimate Plan**
*   **Upsell:** "To keep this data active and searchable by AI, you need the high-performance vector storage included in the **Ultimate** plan." (This pushes them to the higher subscription tier).

---

## 4. Handling Objections

**Objection:** "That's expensive. I can get a clerk to scan it."
**Response:** "A clerk creates 'dumb' PDFs. You can't search them easily, and the AI can't read them accurately without expensive OCR software. We create **Structured Data**. We index it so ALOA can answer questions like 'What was our defense in the 2015 Shell case?'. A clerk cannot give you that."

**Objection:** "What if I leave PracticePro?"
**Response:** "You own your data. We will export the raw PDFs for you. However, the *AI Intelligence Index* (the connections and searchability) is a feature of our platform." *(Note: This reinforces that the value is in the platform, not just the file).*

**Objection:** "I don't want my files leaving the office."
**Response:** "For premium engagements (over 50 boxes), we offer **On-Site Activation**. We bring our scanning team and equipment to your boardroom. This incurs a 20% surcharge."
